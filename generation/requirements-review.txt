Compare the entire original request/requirements with the actual definition, bounds, implementation when supplied, and teaching. Independently enumerate all explicit requirements, including the current problem's requested setting (not the original theme of a reused rule), mechanics, quantities, algorithm combinations versus alternatives, difficulty, style and learning target. The generated contract and author's self-assessment are candidates, not authority to weaken the request. Report missing, weakened, contradictory, infeasible or unsubstantiated requirements with concrete Korean reasons. A correct solution to a simplified problem is not sufficient.
Check each requested algorithm's substantive role and whether a simpler correct approach removes the requested interaction at the declared limits. Recompute time/memory estimates and check meaningful maximum-size cases; do not accept a raw state-space upper bound as proof of necessity, a contrived slow program as difficulty evidence, or a claimed Baekjoon tier as calibration. Do not reject easy tasks for lacking advanced algorithms, force Dijkstra when BFS was allowed, or penalize an equally efficient valid alternative. Large target sets, weights, rotation/time and battery must matter when requested, rather than be decorative or fixed to trivial constants.
For graph compression, verify preservation of time phase, resource state and targets visited along paths, or a sound reset/dominance argument. A scalar distance matrix without that justification can be incorrect. Distinguish exact public coordinate semantics from solution-revealing direction tables. Ensure all stages fit the execution/memory/input limits together. Never repair a mismatch by silently changing the original request.
When requirementsReview exists in the output schema, fill satisfied, coverage (requirement/evidence entries covering the original request), complexity, shortcuts, issues with your own assessment. Use specific references to clauses, code or bounds, not 'all satisfied'. satisfied=true requires no unresolved issues. If fidelity fails, reject the normal review as well (REVISE with issues, or proseEquivalent=false with issues). Keep private requests and learning targets out of learner-facing prose.
